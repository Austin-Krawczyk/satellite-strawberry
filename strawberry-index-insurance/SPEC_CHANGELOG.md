# Spec changelog

Any change to scope in `CLAUDE.md`, dated, one line each.

- 2026-09-15 — Spec created.
- 2026-09-15 — §5 PRISM: `OREGONSTATE/PRISM/AN81d` → `OREGONSTATE/PRISM/ANd`. AN81d is deprecated, last image 2020-12-30, no 2023 data; ANd has the same `ppt` band and 0.0417° grid and covers both 2023 windows. Approved by user.
- 2026-09-15 — §6 Step 1 unit rule, provisional: default changed from "≥ 60% strawberry in both CDL 2022 and 2023" to "≥ 60% in CDL 2023". The both-years rule was meant to filter crop rotation but is filtering CDL 2022 misclassification: of pixels CDL 2023 labels Strawberries, CDL 2022 labels 32% Shrubland and 16% Grass/Pasture, which is not plausible rotation on intensively farmed coastal land; forward agreement (CDL 2022 strawberry → 2023 strawberry) is 90.5%, and only 2 cells were ≥ 60% strawberry in 2022 but not 2023, so real rotation is low. The 15-unit both-years set is kept as a sensitivity check to report in the exhibit. Final rule to be chosen once the user supplies the March 2023 flood extent reference, from unit counts inside and outside the footprint under both rules. Approved by user.
- 2026-09-15 — Validation note on the strawberry-field definition: CDL 2023 class 221 in Monterey + Santa Cruz = 51,124 pixels ≈ 11,368 acres, about 89% of the California Strawberry Commission's 12,728-acre projection for the Watsonville/Salinas district (figure supplied by user). The district also includes some Santa Clara and San Benito County acreage that the two-county filter excludes.
- 2026-09-15 — Data added for evaluation only, not adopted: DWR / Land IQ Statewide Crop Mapping WY2022 and WY2023 (final) file geodatabases in `data/raw/dwr_crop_mapping/` (provenance in SOURCE.md; zips gitignored). Compared with CDL in `notebooks/01b_dwr_check.ipynb`; Step 1 units unchanged. Approved by user.
- 2026-09-15 — §5: DWR / Land IQ Statewide Crop Mapping WY2023 adopted as the source of the analysis units (previously evaluation only). All T20 fields in the two counties uploaded as Earth Engine asset `projects/cropczyk/assets/strawberry_fields_dwr_wy2023`; CDL moved to comparison. Approved by user.
- 2026-09-15 — §6 Step 1: units changed from 250 m CDL cells to DWR WY2023 T20 field polygons with strawberries as the only crop of the water year (sequence A); supersedes the provisional CDL 2023 rule. Reason: only 32.6% of CDL 2023 strawberry area lies inside DWR T20 fields and 54.8% is > 90 m from any, mostly in fields DWR maps as cole crops (37%) or lettuce (34%); 51 of 127 CDL units have < 5% DWR T20 cover and the both-years rule does not fix it (7 of 15); the disagreement is whole fields, not edges, so it is misclassification, not a resolution artefact. Sequence B and C T20 fields stay in the unit table as flagged non-units with crop sequence and T20 peak-greenness attributes. CDL cells (127 and 15) kept as comparisons. Approved by user.
- 2026-09-15 — §6 Step 1: threshold sensitivity (40/50/60% strawberry per 250 m cell) retired, since field boundaries replace gridded cells; results kept in `data/derived/01_threshold_sensitivity.csv`. Approved by user.
- 2026-09-15 — §6 Step 3 and §8: added a planned validation (February 2023 NDVI of sequence A fields vs bare ground, as a check on the assumption that they had a crop in the ground in March 2023) and a crop-map uncertainty limitation (neither DWR nor CDL treated as ground truth; DWR WY2023 main-crop T20 acreage is 132% of the Commission's district projection; DWR ACRES is whole-polygon area vs the Commission's planted acreage; the sequence A assumption is agronomic reasoning). §8 limitation "250 m units vs bed width" reworded to "10–30 m pixels vs bed width". Approved by user.
